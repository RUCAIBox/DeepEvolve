# Website Resources

We collect information from 125 sources across five categories: Science & Technology, Society & Public Affairs, Business & Finance, Culture & Sports, and Energy & Environment. These sources span news outlets, government agencies, official calendars, financial data providers, and specialized information feeds. By integrating diverse and continuously updated sources, we capture emerging events across multiple domains and construct timely, verifiable forecasting questions. This information infrastructure enables systematic evaluation of forecasting agents in a dynamic real-world environment, supporting continuous assessment as new events unfold and ground-truth outcomes become available.
